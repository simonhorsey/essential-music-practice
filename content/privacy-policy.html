--- title: Privacy Policy description: How Essential Music Practice handles your information. section: page ---

This site is run by Simon Horsey. It is designed to collect as little information about you as possible.

What this site does not do

  • No advertising networks.
  • No advertising or social media tracking pixels.
  • No selling or sharing of your details with anyone.

Visitor statistics

The site counts page views using privacy-friendly analytics that do not use cookies and do not identify you.

Email newsletter

If you sign up for the free guide or newsletter, your email address is stored with my email service provider and used only to send you the guide and occasional practice tips. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

Buying the book

Purchases are handled by E-junkie and PayPal. I receive your name and email address so I can deliver your download and help with any problems. I never see your card details.

Contact form

Messages sent through the contact form are used only to reply to you.

Links to other sites

Some articles link to products on other sites, and some of those are affiliate links: if you buy, I may earn a small commission at no cost to you. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Questions

If you want to know what information I hold about you, or want it deleted, contact me.
