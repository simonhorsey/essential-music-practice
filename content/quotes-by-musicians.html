--- title: Quotes by musicians description: A list of quotes by musicians from a variety of backgrounds. I always found it interesting to read other musician quotes and see what they though about music, life and sometimes, other musicians! section: inspiration updated: 2012-11-21 ---

I always found reading quotes by musicians interesting and inspiring when I was learning. I found that reading other musician quotes always gave me a little more insight into their lives and music. It was almost as if reading quotes by musicians about music, performing, or other musicians gave me a small glimpse into their character and, by extension, their personality and lives.

I have put this list of musician's quotes together for those who would like to gain a little deeper insight into some of their favorite composers.

If you know I have mis-attributed a particular musician quote, or you have favorite quotes by musicians of your own that you would like to see on this page please just use the box below to let me know.
