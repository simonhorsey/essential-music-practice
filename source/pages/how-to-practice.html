Everybody wants to know how to practice more effectively. Everyone is trying to work smarter, not harder, and hopefully my free ebook How To Practice Music: 5 Habits You Need To Develop NOW o will help you do just that.

It takes hard work and commitment to learn any musical instrument, but we live in a busy world with many distractions but learning some simple techniques to help make your music practice more efficient and effective will help. 


Work Smarter Not Harder -
Learn How To Practice

The student today needs to work smarter, not necessarily harder. It is all about learning the practice techniques that will help you achieve your musical goals. If you learn good music practice techniques you will find you can get lots more done in less practice time. And if this is the case imagine what would happen to your playing if you got lots more done in the same amount of practice time! 

It's really up to you and your musical goals, but whatever they are, wouldn't you rather be getting more progress out of the time you have available?

Is the 10,000 hour rule real?

Did you know for instance that the much talked about 10,000 hours rule, might just turn out to be not true at all! Well, not in the way that we currently understand it. Neurologists and psychologists are finding new, exciting things about our brains and how they work every single day.

Of course there will always be an element of the 10,000 hours rule that is true because working hard at something will always mean improvement, but what about the starting point? How important is that and, is there actually such a thing as 'natural talent' afterall? More on this soon!
