Know the Notes!

by Brittany Snyder
(McKinney, TX United States)

How this practice game works, is first you and a partner of the same instrument get together. Have the same selection of music and begin the game. How the game works it you and your partner sit facing each other, then what you do is you will see who can learn the first measure the quickest. And this is not timed. Take as much time as you need. But remember, whoever can learn it the quickest, wins.
